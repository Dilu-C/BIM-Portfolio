Philosophy & Production Standard

Draughtsmanship Rigor

My work is grounded in classical Engineering Draughtsmanship discipline. Understanding geometric projection, structural framing load paths, and municipal drawing rules means every 3D element placed in Revit is constructible, coordinated, and ready for site execution.

Unlike operators who rely on surface-level 3D modeling, this technical discipline ensures clean linework, accurate cut planes, and flawless construction documentation.

"Software is only as good as the drafter's understanding of construction reality."

High-Velocity Automation

Architectural production bottlenecks - like batch-processing 57 building keyplans from CAD into Title Blocks, injecting 25+ shared parameters across project models and family hierarchies, or manually laying out hundreds of Door & Window schedule tables - are where human error and project deadlines break down.

By engineering custom pyRevit ribbon suites and AutoCAD MCP automation pipelines, I orchestrate complete end-to-end data pipelines: extracting spatial opening coordinates, auto-mapping parameters to custom generic annotation schedules, and generating 100+ fully-aligned drawing sheets with intelligent 2-row and single-row oversized layouts in seconds.

"Automate the repetitive; focus human intelligence on precision and coordination."
